1. 🎮 About Axe Splitter

Axe Splitter is an intense, action-packed mobile game developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

The game challenges you to become the ultimate axe-throwing champion! Split wooden logs, conquer danger zones, and unlock unique axe skins with special powers:

  • 🔥 Fire Axe — Blazing blade that burns through logs faster
  • ⚡ Thunder Axe — Chain lightning that damages nearby obstacles
  • ❄️ Ice Axe — Freeze effect that slows danger zones
  • 🌑 Shadow Axe — Dark blade that ignores danger zones

Features multiple game modes (Classic, Time Attack, Zen, and Endless), daily rewards, a spin wheel, leaderboards, and challenging levels. It's engaging, thrilling, and completely FREE.

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the Axe Splitter Android application. It describes what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. 📋 Information We Collect

Axe Splitter does not collect personal information. Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device so that your game continues where you left off.

a) Gameplay Data (Stored Locally on Your Device)

  • High scores and best performance
  • Game mode statistics (Classic, Time Attack, Zen, and Endless)
  • Coins collected and earned through gameplay and daily rewards
  • Axe skins unlocked and equipped (Fire, Thunder, Ice, Shadow)
  • Axe upgrade levels and power-ups purchased with in-game coins
  • Daily reward streaks and coins claimed
  • Spin wheel rewards and spins used
  • Level progression and stars earned
  • Tutorial progress and game settings

b) Permissions Used by the App

  • Vibration: Provides haptic feedback during gameplay
  • Internet and network state: Declared by the app. Axe Splitter does not use these to send your gameplay data to any server or third party
✅ What we DON'T collect:

❌ Your name, email, or phone number
❌ Your location or GPS data
❌ Credit card or payment information
❌ Any personally identifiable information
❌ Device identifiers or advertising IDs
❌ Your browsing history or contacts
❌ Any data about your device usage outside this game

  • 🚫 Advertising Identifiers — The app does not use or read your advertising ID

4. 🛡️ No Advertising

Axe Splitter is a completely free game with no advertisements. The game does not show banner, interstitial, rewarded, or any other type of ads.

📌 Important: Axe Splitter does not include any advertising SDK. This includes Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and any other advertising network. No advertising identifier is read, generated, or transmitted by the app.
