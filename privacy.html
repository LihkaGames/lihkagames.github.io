🔒 Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Lihka Games • Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications and games (the "Applications") developed by Lihka Games, and to the Lihka Games website (lihka.in / lihkagames.github.io). We are committed to protecting user privacy and providing a transparent experience.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our Applications are designed to be played and used without the need for user accounts or the collection of personal data.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or share names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise GPS locations.
  • No Financial Data: All Applications are completely free to use. We do not offer in-app purchases and therefore do not request or store any credit card or billing information.
  • No First-Party Tracking in the Applications: We do not use any internal analytics or tracking software to monitor your individual behavior within the Applications.

2. Third-Party Advertising

To support the development and maintenance of these free Applications, we display advertisements from the following third-party networks. These partners may collect and use non-personal data (such as your device's unique Advertising ID, IP address, and device model) to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.

Please review the privacy policies of our advertising partners for more details:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads: https://unity3d.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Children's Privacy

Our Applications do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. We strive to ensure that our advertising partners comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other global safety regulations regarding the delivery of age-appropriate advertisements.

4. Website Analytics

Our website does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information. If you click "Accept" in the consent banner, we load Google Analytics / Firebase to collect anonymous usage information such as pages viewed, device type, browser and approximate location. If you click "Decline", these analytics scripts are not loaded. You can clear your browser's site data to see the banner again and change your choice.

5. Consent

By downloading and using any of our Applications, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
