📱 Overview

Quick Voice Calculator is a free calculator app by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. It lets you perform calculations by speaking, typing, or scanning numbers with your camera. It also includes a scientific calculator mode and an All-in-One Unit Converter (Currency, Length, Area, Volume, Weight, Temperature, Speed, Pressure, Power).

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the controls you have. By installing or using Quick Voice Calculator, you agree to the practices described here.

This policy applies only to the Quick Voice Calculator Android application and its built-in features (voice input, camera scan, history, scientific mode, unit converter).

🔑 Permissions We Request

The app requests only the permissions it needs to function — nothing more.

🎙️
Microphone
Required for the voice calculator feature. Captures audio only while you are actively holding/tapping the mic button — never in the background.
📷
Camera
Used only when you tap the camera icon to scan a printed equation or number. The camera is never accessed without your explicit action.
📁
Storage (optional)
Lets you save or export calculation history/notes locally to your device, if you choose to use that feature.
🌐
Internet Access
Needed to fetch live currency exchange rates and to serve ads. Core calculator functions work fully offline.
✅ You can deny Camera or Microphone permission and still use the basic keypad calculator, scientific mode, and unit converter normally.

📊 Data We Collect

a) Technical & Device Data

  • Device model, OS version, and screen configuration (for compatibility)
  • Advertising ID (anonymous, used for ad delivery)
  • App crash logs and performance diagnostics

b) Usage Data (Anonymous)

  • Feature usage counts (e.g., voice input used X times) — aggregated, not linked to you
  • Calculator mode preference (basic / scientific / converter)

c) Locally Stored Data

  • Calculation history — saved only on your device
  • Unit converter preferences (default units selected)
None of this data is tied to your name, email, or identity. It cannot be used to personally identify you.

🚫 Data We Never Collect

  • ❌ Your name, email address, or phone number
  • ❌ Precise GPS location
  • ❌ Contacts, SMS, or call logs
  • ❌ Photos/media gallery (camera is used live, not gallery access)
  • ❌ Audio recordings stored or uploaded to a server
  • ❌ Payment or banking information — the app has no in-app purchases
  • ❌ Login credentials — there is no account system at all
✅ Bottom line: Quick Voice Calculator is built to calculate, not to collect. We keep it that simple.

🎙️📷 How Voice & Camera Features Work

Voice Input:

  • When you tap the microphone icon, your device's built-in speech-recognition engine (Android Speech-to-Text) converts your voice into numbers/operators.
  • Audio is processed in real time and is not stored or uploaded by Quick Voice Calculator.
  • Recognition may rely on your device's OS-level speech service, which is governed by your device manufacturer's / Google's own privacy policy.

Camera / Scan Input:

  • Tapping the camera icon opens a live camera view to capture a printed number or equation.
  • The captured frame is processed on-device for text/number recognition and is discarded immediately after use — it is not saved to your gallery or sent to any server.
⚠️ We do not retain, transmit, or analyze your voice or camera data beyond the single calculation you're performing in the moment.

💱 Unit Converter & Currency Rates

The All-in-One Unit Converter (Currency, Length, Area, Volume, Weight, Temperature, Speed, Pressure, Power) works mostly offline using standard conversion formulas.

  • Only the Currency converter requires an internet connection, to fetch up-to-date exchange rates from a third-party financial data provider.
  • No personal or financial account information is sent — only the currency codes and amount you want converted (processed locally after rates are fetched).
  • All other converters (Length, Area, Volume, Weight, Temp, Speed, Pressure, Power) work fully offline with no data transmission.

🔒 Data Storage & Security

  • Calculation history and preferences are stored locally in your device's app storage.
  • Any network communication (ads, currency rates) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • We do not operate a central server that stores your personal calculations.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored history and preferences.
⚠️ While we use industry-standard safeguards, no digital system is 100% secure. We encourage keeping your device's OS and security patches up to date.

🧒 Children's Privacy

Quick Voice Calculator is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads served through AdMob follow Google's standard content guidelines.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at support@lihka.in and we will act promptly to remove it.

✅ Your Rights & Choices

  • Revoke Permissions: Disable Camera/Microphone anytime from your device's App Settings.
  • Clear History: Use the in-app option to clear calculation history, or clear app data from device settings.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Available in your device's Google Settings → Ads.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all locally stored data immediately.
  • Request Info: Email us at support@lihka.in to ask what limited data categories apply to you.
