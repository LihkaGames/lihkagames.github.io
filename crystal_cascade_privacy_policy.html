🎮 About Crystal Cascade

Crystal Cascade is a vibrant, addictive crystal-matching game where players tap and cascade colorful crystals to clear the board. With hundreds of challenging levels, daily rewards, and special power-ups, it delivers endless fun for casual gamers of all ages.

Crystal Cascade is proudly developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. We are committed to delivering fun, engaging games while maintaining the highest standards of user privacy and data protection.

This Privacy Policy explains what data Crystal Cascade collects, how we use it, and the rights you have regarding your personal information. We're committed to protecting your privacy while providing a great gaming experience.

ℹ️
By downloading and using Crystal Cascade, you accept this Privacy Policy. If you disagree with any part, please uninstall the game.

📊 Information We Collect

Device & System Information

Advertising ID

Device Details

Model, OS version, screen size, language, and timezone

Network Info

Connection type (WiFi or mobile) and carrier information

Device Identifiers

Unique device IDs for app installation tracking

Gameplay Analytics

Game Progress

Levels completed, high scores, and performance metrics

Crash Reports

App crashes and errors for debugging and improvements

Advertising Data

Ad Impressions

Number of ads shown and when they're displayed

Ad Interactions

Clicks, video completions, and engagement metrics

Targeting Signals

Demographic information for relevant ad delivery

✅
We keep data collection minimal! Only essential information needed to run the game, show ads, and improve gameplay is collected.

No credit cards, bank accounts, or payment details stored

We don't access Facebook, Google, or other social profiles

❌ Contacts & Messages

No access to your contacts, SMS, emails, or conversations

❌ Photos & Media

We never access your photo gallery, videos, or personal files

❌ Biometric Data

No fingerprint, facial recognition, or biometric information

❌ Browser History

We don't track your web browsing or online activity

❌ Calendar & Files

No access to your calendar, reminders, or personal documents

✅
Play worry-free! Crystal Cascade doesn't need personal information to work. Your privacy is safe with us.

Crystal Cascade is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We fully comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and other child protection regulations.

🔐 Data Security & Protection

We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Our Security Measures

🔒 Encryption

HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers

🔒 Secure Storage

Data stored locally on your device using Android's built-in security features

🔒 Limited Retention

Data is automatically deleted after it's no longer needed for its purpose

🔒 Access Control

Only authorized personnel can access data, and only for legitimate purposes

🔒 Regular Audits

We regularly update security practices and respond to new threats
