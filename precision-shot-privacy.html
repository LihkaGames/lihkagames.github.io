🎮 About Precision Shot

Precision Shot is an engaging mobile shooting game where players aim to hit colorful targets with accuracy. Featuring multiple game modes, daily challenges, power-ups, and a shop system, it delivers arcade-style fun for players of all ages.

Precision Shot is proudly developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. We are committed to delivering fun, engaging games while maintaining the highest standards of user privacy and data protection.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Precision Shot application.

ℹ️
By using Precision Shot, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you don't agree with our practices, please don't use the app.

📊 Information We Collect

🔹 Device Identifiers

  • Android Advertising ID (used for ad targeting)
  • Device model, OS version, and screen size
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi or Mobile)
  • Device language and timezone settings

🔹 Gameplay Analytics

  • High scores and performance metrics
  • Game mode preferences and completion rates
  • Level progression and difficulty selected
  • App crashes and performance errors (for debugging)

🔹 Advertising Data

  • Ad impressions and interactions
  • Ad clicks and video completions
  • General demographic targeting signals
✅
We collect the minimum necessary data to provide the game, show relevant ads, and improve performance. Nothing more.

🚫 What We Absolutely Don't Collect

  • ❌ Your name, email address, or phone number
  • ❌ Your precise GPS location or location history
  • ❌ Credit card, payment, or financial information
  • ❌ Social media accounts or personal IDs
  • ❌ Contact lists, SMS messages, or call logs
  • ❌ Photos, videos, or personal media files
  • ❌ Calendar, reminders, or personal schedules
  • ❌ Browser history or web activity tracking
  • ❌ Biometric data (fingerprints, facial recognition)
  • ❌ Any personally identifiable information without consent
✅
Precision Shot respects your privacy completely. Play the game without worrying about your personal data being harvested.

📢 Advertising Networks & Third-Party Services

Precision Shot is free because of advertising revenue. We partner with reputable ad networks to show you relevant ads. Here's who they are:

⚠️
We use 2 advertising networks: Google AdMob and Unity Ads. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies (linked below).
📊

Google AdMob

Google's advertising platform serving banner and interstitial ads throughout the game.

Data AdMob May Access:

  • Google Advertising ID
  • Device model and Android version
  • General location (country/city level)
  • Ad interaction signals
  • Network type and carrier info
📖 Google Privacy Policy ⚙️ Opt Out of Personalized Ads
🎮

Unity Ads

Unity's in-game advertising platform for rewarded video ads and interstitials to monetize the free game.

Data Unity Ads May Access:

  • Unity Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Ad engagement data
  • General location info
  • Network connection type
📖 Unity Privacy Policy
✅
How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:
Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
You can also reset your Advertising ID to receive less targeted ads.

🧒 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Precision Shot is designed for all ages, including children under 13. We fully comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

✓ Ad networks configured for COPPA compliance
