🔎 In Short

Lihka Mobile Print helps you print photos and documents wirelessly to printers on your local Wi-Fi network. The app is built by Lihka (Nagpur, Maharashtra, India). Most of what the app does — reading your files, discovering printers, formatting pages — happens entirely on your device and your local network. We do not read, copy, or upload the content of the files you print.

📄 Local file processing 📶 Local network printer discovery 🚫 No file content uploaded 🔒 No account required 🧒 Safe for all ages

App Permissions & Why We Need Them

Android requires apps to explicitly request permissions. Here's exactly why Lihka Mobile Print asks for each one:

🖼️

Storage / Media Access

Needed to let you browse and select photos and documents from your Gallery and device storage to print them.

📷

Camera

Only used if you choose to capture a new photo directly within the app to print immediately.

📶

Wi-Fi / Network State

Required to detect whether you're connected to Wi-Fi and to discover printers available on that same network.

🛰️

Nearby Devices (Local Network)

Used to discover printers via mDNS/UPnP/Bonjour protocols on your local network — no internet-based scanning.

🌐

Internet

Used for app updates, optional crash reporting, and loading ads (if applicable) — not for transmitting your print content.

💾

Write to External Storage

Used to save print settings, recent print history, and temporary print previews on your device.

⚠️ Good to know: You can deny or revoke any permission in Android Settings → Apps → Lihka Mobile Print → Permissions at any time. Some features (like printing a gallery photo) simply won't work without the related permission.
3

Printer & Local Network Access

Lihka Mobile Print connects to printers using standard local networking protocols, such as:

  • IPP (Internet Printing Protocol) over your local Wi-Fi
  • mDNS/Bonjour printer discovery
  • Direct IP:Port connection (e.g. Web Service printers like 192.168.1.8 | Port 80)
  • Standard Android Print Framework integration

All communication with your printer happens over your local Wi-Fi network — your print files are never routed through Lihka's servers or any cloud service.

✅ No Cloud Printing: Lihka Mobile Print does not upload your files to a remote print server. Everything happens device-to-printer, on the network you control.
4

How We Use Information

The limited, non-content data we may receive is used strictly for:

  • Detecting and listing available printers on your network
  • Remembering your last-used print settings (paper size, color mode, orientation, copies)
  • Maintaining your "Recent Prints" list locally on your device
  • Diagnosing app crashes to fix bugs and improve stability
  • Validating PRO feature purchases through Google Play Billing
  • Showing non-intrusive ads (if the free version includes them) to support development
🚫 We do NOT: read the contents of your documents or photos, build a profile of what you print, sell any data to advertisers, or track your location.
5

Data Storage & Security

Your print settings, recent print history, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's secure app-sandboxed storage. We apply the following safeguards:

  • 🔐 Files are processed in-memory and sent directly to the printer — not duplicated to external servers
  • 🔐 Local network traffic uses standard printer protocols (IPP/mDNS) that stay within your Wi-Fi network
  • 🔐 Any diagnostic data transmitted is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • 🔐 No print content is cached on remote servers
⚠️ Note: While we take reasonable precautions, no method of digital storage or wireless transmission is 100% secure. Use trusted, password-protected Wi-Fi networks when printing sensitive documents.

Third-Party Services

Lihka Mobile Print may integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services — app distribution, updates, and PRO purchase validation
  • Google Play Billing — securely processes any in-app PRO purchases; we never see your payment details
  • Firebase Crashlytics (optional) — anonymous crash and performance reporting to help us fix bugs
  • Google AdMob (free version only, if applicable) — may display banner/interstitial ads; subject to Google's Privacy Policy

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not control or have access to personally identifiable data collected independently by these providers.
