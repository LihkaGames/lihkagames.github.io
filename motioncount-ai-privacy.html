01

⦿ Overview

MotionCount AI (published as Motion Detection Counter) is an intelligent movement-tracking application developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. The app uses your device's camera together with on-device AI (TensorFlow Lite) to detect motion in real time, and ML Kit's OCR engine to optionally verify page/reference numbers during scanning sessions.

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app touches, how it's processed, and what control you have over it. By installing or using MotionCount AI, you agree to the practices described below.

🔍 Core principle: MotionCount AI is engineered to do all of its "thinking" directly on your device. Motion detection and OCR scanning never require sending your camera feed to our servers or anyone else's.
02

⦿ Data We Collect

We keep data collection minimal and tied directly to app functionality:

  • Camera feed (temporary, in-memory): used live to detect motion via GPU-accelerated TensorFlow Lite — never saved as photos/videos unless you explicitly export a scan.
  • Scan statistics: motion counts, scan duration, sensitivity level used, stored locally in your scan history.
  • App preferences: motion threshold %, AI mode toggle, OCR verification toggle, sound/haptic settings — stored locally on your device.
  • Device diagnostics: crash logs, device model, OS version — used only to fix bugs and improve performance.
🧠 None of the above includes your name, email, phone number, or any identity information — the app doesn't ask for any of it.
03

⦿ On-Device AI Engine

MotionCount AI's detection pipeline is built to run entirely on your device's hardware:

  • TensorFlow Lite (GPU Accelerated): analyzes live camera frames locally to detect and count motion instantly.
  • ML Kit OCR Verification: when enabled, reads visible page numbers / text locally using Google's on-device ML Kit library — no text or images leave your phone for this process.
  • Motion Threshold Engine: the sensitivity slider (Low–High) adjusts detection locally; this value never leaves your device.
✅ Because processing happens on-device, your camera frames are analyzed in real time and immediately discarded — they are not stored, transmitted, or shared in any form during normal use.
04

⦿ App Permissions

MotionCount AI requests only the permissions essential to its features:

📷 Camera Required to detect motion and run OCR verification in real time. Feed is processed locally and not recorded by default.
📳 Vibration (Haptic Feedback) Used to give tactile feedback when motion is detected or a scan completes. Can be disabled anytime in Preferences.
💾 App-Private Storage Used to save your scan history and settings locally in the app's private sandbox — not accessible to other apps.
You can review or revoke Camera and Vibration permissions anytime via Android Settings → Apps → MotionCount AI → Permissions.
05

⦿ What We Don't Collect

To be fully transparent, here's a direct comparison:

✔ Collected (minimal)

▪ Local scan counts & history
▪ App settings/preferences
▪ Anonymous crash/diagnostic logs
▪ Device model & OS version

✘ Never Collected

▪ Precise GPS / location
▪ Photos, videos or media library
▪ Contacts, SMS or call logs

06

⦿ Local Storage & Scan History

Your "Scans" count, history entries, and preferences shown on the home screen (e.g. 0 Scans · History) are stored in a local database inside the app's private storage container on your device.

  • This data stays on your device and is never synced to any cloud server by default.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this local history.
  • You can manually clear scan history anytime from within the app's Settings.
07

⦿ Advertising & Third-Party Services LIMITED

MotionCount AI may display ads or use lightweight analytics/crash-reporting tools to keep the app free and stable. If and where used, these services are limited to industry-standard providers such as:

  • Google AdMob — may serve banner/interstitial ads using a resettable Advertising ID. View Google's Privacy Policy →
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs. View Google's Privacy Policy →
These services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect basic technical identifiers (Advertising ID, device model, IP-based region). Lihka does not receive any personally identifiable information from them.
08

⦿ Data Security

  • 🔐 All local data is sandboxed within the app's private Android storage.
  • 🔐 No camera footage is written to disk during normal motion-detection use.
  • 🔐 Any network calls (e.g. ad requests, crash logs) use encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • 🔐 Regular app updates patch known vulnerabilities.
⚠️ No digital system is 100% immune to risk. While we apply strong safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data in transit or storage.
09

⦿ Children's Privacy

MotionCount AI is a general-audience utility app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. No account creation, chat, or social features exist within the app. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us at support@lihka.in for prompt removal.

⦿ Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: View your scan history anytime directly inside the app.
  • Delete: Clear local history or uninstall the app to erase all locally stored data.
  • Permission Control: Revoke Camera/Vibration access anytime via device settings.
  • Ad Personalization: Opt out via Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.
  • Inquiries: Email us to ask what limited diagnostic data, if any, is associated with your device.
