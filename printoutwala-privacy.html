1. 🏢 About Printout Wala

Printout Wala is a home delivery printout service app developed and operated by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

We provide doorstep delivery of the following print services:

  • 📄 Black & White Printouts (Single-Side / Double-Side)
  • 🎨 Color Printouts (Single-Side / Double-Side)
  • 📸 Passport Size Photos (12 copies per order)
  • 💳 PVC Card Printing
This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the Printout Wala Android application. It describes what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. 📋 Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Phone number (for order communication and delivery)
  • Delivery address

b) Device Information

  • Device ID — used to generate your unique Delivery PIN for order verification
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data)

c) Files and Documents You Upload

  • PDF, Word (.doc/.docx), Excel (.xls/.xlsx), PowerPoint (.ppt/.pptx), and image files (JPG, PNG, etc.) that you select for printing
  • Files are used exclusively for completing your print order
  • Files are automatically and permanently deleted within 24 hours of upload

d) Location Information

  • Your GPS location — used only when you place an order to enable accurate delivery to your address
  • Location data is not stored permanently after the order is fulfilled

3. 📱 Permissions We Request & Why

We request only permissions that are strictly necessary for the app to function. Here is a full breakdown:

🌐 INTERNET
Required to upload your documents to our server, place orders, receive real-time order status updates, and load advertisement content.
📍 ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Required to detect your current GPS location so we can deliver your printouts to your exact address. Location is accessed only when you tap "Detect My Location" while placing an order. It is not tracked in the background.
📁 READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED
Required to allow you to select documents and images from your device storage for printing. We only read files you explicitly select — we do NOT scan, index, or access any other files on your device.
💾 WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 9 & below only
Required on older Android versions to save temporary processing files before upload. Not used or requested on Android 10 and above.
📷 CAMERA
Required to allow you to take a photo directly within the app for passport photo or PVC card orders, as an alternative to selecting from your gallery.
📶 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
Required to check your internet connection status before uploading files. This ensures a smooth upload experience and alerts you if you go offline during an upload.
⚙️ FOREGROUND_SERVICE
Required to run background services that handle automatic deletion of your uploaded files after 24 hours, and to maintain a local web server used for secure file transfer within the app.
🔔 POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Required to send you real-time order status push notifications, such as: Order Placed ✅, Printing Started 🖨️, Out for Delivery 🛵, and Order Delivered 📦.
📞 CALL_PHONE
Required to allow you to directly call our support number (+91 7350157047) from within the app using the "Call Support" button, without leaving the app.
📱 READ_PHONE_STATE & READ_PHONE_NUMBERS
Required to read your SIM phone number(s) so you can conveniently select your number for order communication without typing it manually. Also used to generate a unique Device ID for your secure Delivery PIN. We do not store or transmit your phone number through this permission without your explicit action.
📳 VIBRATE
Required to provide haptic feedback with notifications, alerting you to order status changes even when your phone is on silent mode.
📢 AD_ID Advertising Only
Required by Google AdMob and Unity Ads to serve relevant advertisements. This is an anonymous Advertising ID that you can reset or opt out of in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. 🔑 Delivery PIN — Important Security Notice

⚠️ Your Delivery PIN is Strictly Confidential.

Your Delivery PIN is automatically generated from your unique Device ID and is used only to verify your identity at the time of delivery. The delivery person will ask you to show your PIN before handing over your order.

🚫 Never share your Delivery PIN with anyone, including people claiming to be our support staff. Our team will never ask for your Delivery PIN via call, WhatsApp, or any other channel.

6. 🗂️ Document & File Handling Policy

When you upload a document or image for printing, the following rules apply:

🔒 Secure Storage
Files are stored on our secured server only for the duration needed to complete your print order.
👁️ Restricted Access
Only authorized printing staff can access your files, and solely for the purpose of printing your order.
🗑️ Automatic Deletion — 24 Hours
All uploaded files are automatically and permanently deleted within 24 hours of upload, regardless of whether the order was completed or cancelled. This process runs automatically and cannot be reversed.
🚫 No Cloud Backup or Sharing
Your files are never backed up to any cloud storage service (such as Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.) and are never shared with any third party under any circumstances.
✅ File Privacy Guarantee: Your documents are your private property. We treat them with the highest level of confidentiality and delete them automatically within 24 hours.

7. 📢 Advertising — Google AdMob & Unity Ads

Printout Wala is a free app. To support the cost of development and free delivery service, we display banner advertisements within the app. Ads are shown only to Customer users. Admin and Delivery personnel do not see any advertisements.

📌 Important: We use exactly 2 advertising networks in Printout Wala: Google AdMob and Unity Ads. We do not use any other ad networks in this app.

What data do these ad networks collect?
These third-party ad networks may collect and use the following non-personal, anonymous data from your device to serve relevant ads and prevent ad fraud:

📊

Google AdMob

Google's mobile advertising platform. AdMob may collect anonymous data including your Advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version, and general location (country/city) to serve relevant banner ads.

Data AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (resettable by you in device settings)
  • IP address (used for geo-targeting — country level only)
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • App interaction signals (ad views, clicks)
  • Network type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data)
📖 View Google's Privacy Policy →    ⚙️ Opt Out of Personalized Ads →
🎮

Unity Ads

Unity Technologies' advertising platform. Unity Ads may collect anonymous data including your Advertising ID, device information, and app usage signals to serve relevant banner ads.

Data Unity Ads may collect:

  • Advertising ID (resettable by you in device settings)
  • IP address (for geo-targeting — country level only)
  • Device model, OS version, and screen size
  • App session information
  • Ad interaction signals (views, clicks)
📖 View Unity's Privacy Policy →    ⚙️ Unity Ads Opt-Out →
✅ How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

Doing this does not stop ads from showing, but ads will be less personalized (you will see generic ads instead).
⚠️ Note on Ad Data: The data collected by Google AdMob and Unity Ads is governed entirely by their own privacy policies (linked above). Lihka does not have access to, control over, or receive any personally identifiable information from these ad networks. We only receive aggregate revenue reports (total earnings — no user-level data).
