1. 📄 About Lihka Scan: Quick PDF Scanner

2. 🛡️ Our Key Privacy Principles

Everything we build in Lihka Scan is guided by these core privacy principles:

  • ✅ Local-First Processing: All scanning, PDF editing, conversion, compression, password protection, digital signing, OCR, and QR scanning are performed entirely on your device. Your documents never leave your phone through our app.
  • ✅ Minimal Data Collection: We collect only the minimum anonymous device data required for the app to function and display advertisements. We do not collect your name, email, or personal account information.
  • ✅ No Cloud Upload: Lihka Scan does not upload your scanned files, PDFs, or images to any server — ours or any third party's.
  • ✅ No Personal Profiling: We do not build personal profiles of users. We do not track what documents you scan or what content is in your files.
  • ✅ Transparency: Every permission we request is explained clearly in this policy. We use no hidden permissions or background tracking.

3. 📋 Information We Collect

a) Information We Do NOT Collect

✅ We do NOT collect:

• Your name, email address, or phone number
• The content of your scanned documents or PDFs
• Photos or images you scan or import
• Text extracted through OCR
• QR code contents you scan
• ID card or passport information
• Your document library or file names
• Your passwords set on PDF files
• Your digital signature drawing
None of this data is transmitted from your device.

b) Anonymous Device Information (Collected Automatically)

The following minimal, anonymous technical data may be collected automatically for the sole purpose of app stability monitoring and displaying advertisements:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Android OS version
  • App version number
  • Anonymous Advertising ID (resettable by you)
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data) — for ad loading only
  • General geographic region (country/city level — for ads only)
  • App crash logs (anonymous — no personal data included)

c) No Account Required

Lihka Scan requires no registration, no login, no email address, and no personal account of any kind. The app functions fully based on your device's local storage.

📌 Important: Your scanned documents, PDFs, images, and any files you create or manage within Lihka Scan are stored entirely on your device's local storage. Lihka does not have access to, cannot view, and does not transmit any of your document content.

4. 📱 Permissions We Request & Why

We request only permissions strictly necessary for the app features to function. Here is a complete and transparent breakdown of every permission used in Lihka Scan:

📷 CAMERA
Required for: Document scanning (Quick Scan feature), ID Card Scan, Passport Scan, QR Code Scanner, and OCR Text (capture from camera). The camera is accessed only when you actively use a scanning feature. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, stored remotely, or transmitted to any server.
🖼️ READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Required for: Accessing photos and images from your device gallery for features like Image to PDF, Image Filters, OCR Text (import from gallery), ID Card Scan (import from gallery), and Passport Scan (import from gallery). We read only the files you explicitly select. We do not scan or browse your entire storage automatically.
📁 READ_MEDIA_ALL / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (if applicable)
Required for: Accessing PDF files from your device storage for tools like Merge PDFs, Compress PDF, Extract Pages, Rotate Pages, Reorder Pages, Add/Remove Password, PDF to Image, PDF to Text, PDF Color Inverter, and File Compressor. You always manually select which files to open — we never auto-browse your storage.
💾 WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Required for: Saving your scanned PDFs, converted files, compressed files, merged documents, and all output files to your device storage. Files are saved to your chosen or default location on your device. No files are saved to any remote server.
🌐 INTERNET
Required for: Loading banner advertisements from Google AdMob (to support the free app). Core scanning, PDF editing, conversion, compression, OCR, QR scanning, and all document tools work fully offline without an internet connection. Internet is only required for ad display.
📶 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Required for: Checking your internet connection status before attempting to load advertisements. This prevents unnecessary loading errors when the device is offline.
📢 AD_ID Advertising Only
Required for: Google AdMob to serve relevant banner advertisements within the app. This is an anonymous, resettable Advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out of personalized ads anytime via: Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.
🔔 POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)
Required for: Displaying progress notifications when processing large PDF operations (compression, merging, conversion) that run in the background so you can continue using your phone while the task completes.
📳 VIBRATE
Required for: Providing haptic feedback when a QR code is successfully scanned, and when certain processing operations complete.
⚠️ Note: Lihka Scan does not request or use Location (GPS), Contacts, Microphone, Phone State, SMS, or any permission not listed above. If you see a permission request not listed here, please contact us immediately at support@lihka.in.

5. 🪪 Sensitive Document Features — Special Notice

Lihka Scan includes special scanning features for sensitive identity documents. Please read the following important notices carefully:

🪪 ID Card Scanner

The ID Card Scanner feature allows you to scan the front and back of identity cards (Aadhaar, PAN, Driver's License, etc.) and export them as a PDF.

Privacy Guarantee: All ID card image processing is performed 100% on your device. The images you capture are never transmitted to Lihka's servers, Google (beyond what the camera/storage permission normally handles), or any other third party. The resulting PDF is saved only to your device's local storage.

⚠️ User Responsibility: You are responsible for keeping your scanned ID documents secure on your device. We strongly recommend setting a password on any PDF containing identity documents using the Add Password feature built into Lihka Scan. Lihka is not responsible for unauthorized access to documents stored on your device.

🛂 Passport Scanner

The Passport Scanner feature allows you to scan your passport's bio-data page and visa stamps, with MRZ (Machine Readable Zone) line awareness, and export as a secure PDF.

Privacy Guarantee: Passport image capture and PDF generation is performed entirely on your device. No passport data, MRZ data, or personal information from your passport is read, extracted, stored, or transmitted by Lihka Scan beyond saving the image/PDF to your device storage.

⚠️ User Responsibility: Passport documents are highly sensitive. Please use the Password Protection feature and keep your device secure. Do not share passport PDFs through insecure channels.

🔡 OCR Text — Extract Text from Image

The OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature, powered by Google ML Kit, allows you to extract text from images and documents in 50+ languages.

Privacy Guarantee: OCR processing via Google ML Kit runs on-device. The image content you scan for OCR is not sent to Google's servers — Google ML Kit's on-device model performs all recognition locally. No text content extracted by OCR is collected or transmitted by Lihka.

📷 QR Code Scanner

The QR Scanner feature scans QR codes quickly and securely using your device camera.

Privacy Guarantee: QR code scanning is performed entirely offline on your device. The content of QR codes you scan is never transmitted to Lihka or any third party. QR scan history is stored only locally on your device.

✍️ Digital Signature

The Digital Signature feature allows you to draw your signature using ink, color, and width controls, and apply it to documents.

Privacy Guarantee: Your digital signature is created and stored entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to any server. You control when and where to apply your signature.

7. 🛠️ PDF Tools & Features — Privacy Details

The following summarizes how each major tool category handles your data from a privacy perspective:

📦 PDF Tools (Merge, Extract, Compress, Rotate, Reorder)
All PDF manipulation tools operate entirely on your device using local processing libraries. Your PDF files are opened from and saved back to your device storage. No PDF content is transmitted to any server.
🔒 Security (Add Password, Remove Password, Digital Sign)
Password encryption and decryption of PDF files is performed entirely on your device. Your passwords are never stored or transmitted by Lihka Scan. Digital signatures are created and applied locally. No security data leaves your device.
🔄 Convert (PDF to Text, Text to PDF, PDF to Image, Image to PDF, QR Scanner, Share as Long Image)
All conversion operations are performed locally on your device. Input files are read from device storage, processed on-device, and output files are saved to your device storage. No content is transmitted externally during conversion.
🎨 Edit (Image Filters, PDF Color Inverter / Filter Studio)
Image and PDF editing, filter application, and color inversion are performed entirely on-device. Your original files and edited outputs remain on your device only.
🪪 Scan Special (ID Card Scan, Passport Scan, OCR Text)
These sensitive scanning features use on-device processing only. See Section 5 for detailed privacy guarantees for each feature.
🗜️ Utilities (File Compressor)
File compression is performed locally on your device. Input and output files remain on your device storage throughout the process.
📁 My Files & My Folders
Your document library, folder structure (Documents, Notes, Photos, Receipts, and custom folders), and file organization are managed and stored entirely on your device. Lihka does not sync or upload your library to any cloud or server.

9. 🔒 Data Security

We take data security seriously. The following technical and organizational measures are in place to protect your privacy:

  • 🔐 All document processing is performed locally — no data transmission of document content
  • 🔐 The app does not maintain any user accounts or user databases on our servers
  • 🔐 Only minimal anonymous diagnostic data (crash logs) may be transmitted securely via HTTPS
  • 🔐 No third-party tracking SDKs beyond our disclosed Google AdMob
  • 🔐 No analytics SDKs that profile your behavior within the app
  • 🔐 App-level PDF password protection is available for your sensitive files
  • 🔐 All ad communication uses secure HTTPS connections
⚠️ Device Security Reminder: Since all your scanned documents and PDFs are stored locally on your device, the security of those files depends on your device security settings. We strongly recommend:
  • Setting a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or biometric) on your device
  • Using the Password Protection feature for sensitive PDF documents
  • Not sharing your device with unauthorized persons when sensitive documents are stored
  • Regularly backing up and deleting sensitive scanned documents when no longer needed

10. 💾 Data Storage & Retention

Your Documents (Scans, PDFs, Files):

  • Stored entirely on your device's local storage
  • Retained until you delete them manually within the app or via your device file manager
  • Deleted automatically when you uninstall the app (for app-specific storage)
  • Lihka has no access to or copy of any of your documents

Anonymous Diagnostic Data:

  • Anonymous crash logs — retained for a short period to diagnose and fix bugs, then deleted
  • No personally identifiable information is included in crash logs

Advertising Data:

  • Advertising data is managed by Google AdMob per Google's data retention policies
  • Your Advertising ID can be reset by you at any time in your device settings
✅ Complete Data Deletion: To completely remove all data associated with Lihka Scan from your device:
  • Delete your scanned files from within the app or your file manager
  • Clear app data: Settings → Apps → Lihka Scan → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app from your device
Since we do not maintain any server-side user data, uninstalling the app removes all associated data completely.
