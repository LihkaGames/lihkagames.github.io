1. 🏢 About Stationery Wala

Stationery Wala is a home delivery stationery app developed and operated by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

We provide fast doorstep delivery of all stationery items including:

  • ✏️ Pens, Pencils, Gel Pens & Markers
  • 📓 Notebooks, Registers & Files
  • 📐 Geometry Boxes, Rulers & Scales
  • 🎨 Sketch Pens, Crayons & Drawing Supplies
  • 📎 Staplers, Punching Machines & Office Supplies
  • 🎁 Gift Items & Envelopes
  • 📄 A4 Paper, A3 Paper & Printing Sheets
  • 🖊️ All other stationery and school/office supplies
This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the Stationery Wala Android application. It describes what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

a) Personal Information

3. 📱 Permissions We Request & Why

We request only permissions strictly necessary for the app to function. Here is a full breakdown of every permission we use:

🌐 INTERNET
Required to browse stationery products, place orders, receive real-time order status updates, communicate with our servers, and load advertisement content from Google AdMob.
📍 ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Required to detect your current GPS location so we can deliver your stationery order to your exact address in Nagpur. Location is accessed only when you place an order or use the "Detect My Location" feature. It is NOT tracked in the background.
📶 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
Required to check your internet connection status before placing orders. This ensures a smooth ordering experience and alerts you if your device is offline while browsing or ordering.
🔔 POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Required to send you real-time order status push notifications, such as: Order Placed ✅, Order Confirmed 📦, Out for Delivery 🛵, and Order Delivered 🎉.
📞 CALL_PHONE
Required to allow you to directly call our support number (+91 7350157047) from within the app using the "Call Support" button in the Support tab.
📱 READ_PHONE_STATE
Required to generate a unique Device ID that is used to create your secure Delivery PIN. This PIN verifies your identity when the delivery person arrives at your doorstep. We do not store or transmit your phone number through this permission.
📳 VIBRATE
Required to provide haptic feedback with order notifications, alerting you to order status changes even when your phone is on silent mode.
📢 AD_ID Advertising Only
Required by Google AdMob to serve relevant banner advertisements within the app. This is an anonymous Advertising ID that you can reset or opt out of in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. 🔑 Delivery PIN — Important Security Notice

⚠️ Your Delivery PIN is Strictly Confidential.

Your Delivery PIN is automatically generated from your unique Device ID and is used only to verify your identity at the time of delivery. The delivery person will ask you to show your PIN before handing over your stationery order.

🚫 Never share your Delivery PIN with anyone, including people claiming to be our support staff. Our team will never ask for your Delivery PIN via call, WhatsApp, or any other channel.

The Delivery PIN is visible on your order card in the My Orders screen of the app.

6. 🚚 Order & Delivery Policy

When you place a stationery order through Stationery Wala, the following rules apply:

📍 Delivery Area
Stationery Wala currently delivers within Nagpur, Maharashtra only. Delivery availability is shown based on your pincode when placing an order.
🚀 Delivery Time
Orders are typically delivered the same day, between 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Delivery time may vary based on your location within Nagpur, order volume, and product availability.
💰 Delivery Charges
A delivery charge of ₹30 applies per order. FREE delivery on orders above ₹399. Applicable charges are clearly shown before you confirm your order.
💵 Payment
We currently accept Cash on Delivery (COD) only. Pay conveniently when your order arrives at your doorstep. No online payment or pre-payment is required.
❌ Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched. Once the order is out for delivery, cancellation is not possible. To cancel, contact us immediately at +91 7350157047.
🔄 Returns & Refunds
If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please report it within 24 hours of delivery with photo proof by WhatsApp to +91 7350157047. We will arrange a replacement or refund after verification. Custom or used items are not eligible for return.
✅ Order Privacy: Your order details, delivery address, and personal information are shared only with our delivery team strictly for the purpose of completing your order. They are never shared with any third party.

7. 📢 Advertising — Google AdMob

Stationery Wala is a free app. To support the cost of development and our delivery service, we display banner advertisements within the app. Ads are shown only to Customer users. Admin and Delivery personnel do not see any advertisements.

📌 Important: We use exactly 1 advertising network in Stationery Wala: Google AdMob only. We do not use Unity Ads or any other ad network in this app.

What data does Google AdMob collect?
Google AdMob may collect and use the following non-personal, anonymous data from your device to serve relevant ads and prevent ad fraud:

📊

Google AdMob

Google's mobile advertising platform. AdMob may collect anonymous data including your Advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version, and general location (country/city) to serve relevant banner ads within Stationery Wala.

Data AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (resettable by you in device settings)
  • IP address (used for geo-targeting — country level only)
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • App interaction signals (ad views, clicks)
  • Network type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data)
📖 View Google's Privacy Policy →    ⚙️ Opt Out of Personalized Ads →
✅ How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

Doing this does not stop ads from showing, but ads will be less personalized (you will see generic ads instead).
⚠️ Note on Ad Data: The data collected by Google AdMob is governed entirely by Google's own privacy policy (linked above). Lihka does not have access to, control over, or receive any personally identifiable information from Google AdMob. We only receive aggregate revenue reports (total earnings — no user-level data is shared with us).
