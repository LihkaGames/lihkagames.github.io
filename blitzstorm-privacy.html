1. ⚡ About BlitzStorm Arena

BlitzStorm Arena is a fast-paced arena combat game developed by Game Studio AM.

Battle through Campaign and Survival modes, collect and upgrade weapons (Rifles, SMGs, Shotguns, Snipers, Pistols, LMGs, and Special Weapons), unlock heroes, complete achievements, and climb the leaderboard on your device. The game is FREE TO PLAY.

📴 Offline Game: BlitzStorm Arena does not require an internet connection and does not use one. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no online services.
This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the BlitzStorm Arena Android application. It describes what information the game stores, why it is stored, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. 📋 Information We Store

All game information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

a) Gameplay Progress and Statistics

  • Player display name (shown on the local leaderboard)
  • High scores, kills, and battle statistics
  • Campaign level progress and stars earned
  • Coins, gems, and lives
  • Weapons and heroes unlocked and selected
  • Achievement progress
  • Lucky Spin and daily reward status

b) Settings

  • Music, sound effects, vibration, and dark mode preferences

c) Device Permissions

VIBRATE
Used only to vibrate your phone for in-game feedback. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

The game requests no other permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or network.

✅ What we DON'T collect:

❌ Your name, email, or phone number
❌ Your real-world location or GPS data
❌ Credit card or payment information
❌ Any personally identifiable information
❌ Your contacts, messages, photos, or browsing history
❌ Advertising IDs or device identifiers used for advertising
❌ Crash reports or usage analytics

3. 🚫 What We DON'T Collect

To be clear, BlitzStorm Arena does NOT collect or transmit:

  • 🚫 Personal Information: We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, or any personal details
  • 🚫 Financial Data: The game has no purchases. No payment information is ever requested or stored
  • 🚫 Location Data: We don't access your GPS, location services, or real-world whereabouts
  • 🚫 Contact Information: We never access your contacts, SMS messages, or call logs
  • 🚫 Photos or Media: We don't access your photos, videos, or media library
  • 🚫 Analytics or Tracking: We don't use analytics, tracking, or profiling of any kind
  • 🚫 Advertising Data: The game shows no ads and does not read your Advertising ID
  • 🚫 Account Credentials: You don't need to create or log in to any account
✅ Result: BlitzStorm Arena respects your privacy. Just download, play, compete, and enjoy. No strings attached.

4. 📴 No Advertising

BlitzStorm Arena contains no advertisements. The app does not include any advertising SDK, ad network, or ad identifier. You will not see banner, interstitial, rewarded, or app-open ads.

📌 Ad-free: No ad networks are used in BlitzStorm Arena, and no advertiser receives any data from the game.

5. 📂 How We Use Your Information

Information stored on your device is used only to run the game:

  • ✅ Save your progress, unlocks, coins, and settings between sessions
  • ✅ Show your statistics and achievements on your device
  • ✅ Display the local leaderboard
  • ✅ Track daily reward and Lucky Spin timing
  • ✅ Provide vibration feedback when you enable it
🚫 We NEVER:

• Sell your data to any third party
• Send your data off your device
• Use your data for anything other than running the game
• Track your location or background activity
• Use your data for profiling or discrimination

6. 🧒 Children's Privacy

BlitzStorm Arena features action-packed combat gameplay and is appropriate for players of all ages.

✅ Child Safety Measures:

• The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children
• The game contains no advertising
• The game works offline and sends no data over the internet
• No account registration is required
• No in-app purchases
• The game has no chat or contact with other players
• The leaderboard is stored on your device and shows only the display name you use in the game

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about this policy, contact us at support@lihka.in. Because the game does not send personal information to us, you can remove all game data at any time by using Reset Progress in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. 🔗 Third-Party Services

BlitzStorm Arena does not include any third-party SDKs or services that collect data from the game.

  • Google Play: If you download or update the app from Google Play, Google may process information according to its own Privacy Policy. The game itself does not send any information to Google.
  • Links: The Privacy Policy link in the game opens this page in your web browser only when you tap it. The game does not otherwise connect to the internet.

8. 🔒 Data Security

Your data is protected by the design of the game:

  • 🔐 Progress and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access
  • 🔐 The game does not transmit any data over the network
  • 🔐 Cloud backup of app data is disabled
  • 🔐 No third-party services have access to your data
  • 🔐 Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data
⚠️ Limitation: Anyone with physical access to your unlocked device may be able to view information on the screen. Keep your device secured with a screen lock.

9. ✏️ Your Privacy Rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly:

  • 📋 Access: All of your progress and statistics are visible within the game
  • 🗑️ Deletion: Use Reset Progress in Settings, or uninstall the app, to delete all stored data
  • 🔊 Settings: Turn music, sound effects, and vibration on or off at any time

For any other questions about your data, contact us at support@lihka.in. We will respond within 7 business days.

10. 🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Continued use of BlitzStorm Arena after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
