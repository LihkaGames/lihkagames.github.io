🏁 About Turbo Trail Racer Game Info

Turbo Trail Racer is a fast-paced hill-climb style racing game developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

Control your vehicle's Gas and Brake across bumpy off-road trails, manage your Fuel meter, protect your Hearts (Lives), and race to the finish line while collecting coins along the track.

Unlock and upgrade vehicles in the Garage/Shop — from Sports and Muscle cars to Supercars, Legendary rides, and Bikes — each with its own Speed (SPD), Acceleration (ACC), and Grip (GRP) stats. Climb the global Leaderboard, complete Missions, unlock Achievements, spin the Lucky Spin wheel, open the Lucky Box, and collect Daily Rewards for free coins.

🚗 Garage & Shop 🏆 Leaderboard 🎖️ Achievements 🎡 Lucky Spin 🎁 Lucky Box 📅 Daily Rewards 📋 Missions
This Privacy Policy applies only to the Turbo Trail Racer Android application. It explains what information is collected, how it's used, and the controls available to you. By playing the game, you agree to the terms below.

📋 Information We Collect Data

a) Device Information

  • Device Advertising ID (used for ad personalization)
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data)

b) Gameplay Data (Anonymous)

  • Vehicles unlocked, equipped, and upgraded (Garage progress)
  • Coins earned/spent, race distance, and best scores
  • Level progress, stars earned, and mission completions
  • Lucky Spin results and Lucky Box openings
  • Daily Rewards streak and claim history
  • Achievements unlocked and Leaderboard rank
  • Crash reports and app performance metrics

c) Advertising Identifiers

  • Google Advertising ID — used by ad partners to serve relevant ads
  • Fully anonymous and resettable anytime from device settings
✅ None of this data includes your name, email, phone number, GPS location, or any personally identifiable information.

🚫 What We DON'T Collect Zero Data

Turbo Trail Racer does NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Financial or payment card details (Google Play handles purchases securely, if any)
  • GPS location or real-time whereabouts
  • Contacts, SMS messages, or call logs
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Calendar entries or personal reminders
  • Login credentials — no account required to race
✅ Just install, hit the gas, and race. No signup, no personal strings attached.

🧒 Children's Privacy COPPA

Turbo Trail Racer is suitable for players of all ages, including children.

  • No personal information is knowingly collected from children under 13
  • Google AdMob is configured for COPPA compliance
  • No account registration is required to play
  • Coins, vehicles, and rewards are earned purely through gameplay
  • Leaderboard shows usernames only — never personal details
If you're a parent/guardian and believe your child shared personal data, contact us at support@lihka.in for prompt removal.
