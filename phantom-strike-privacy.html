1. 🎯 About Phantom Strike

Phantom Strike is a tactical stealth-action puzzle shooter developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

Guide your agent across grid-based levels using dual joysticks — one to Move, one to Aim & Shoot — while eliminating hostile targets and avoiding enemy fire. Collect diamonds, earn coins, and equip tactical power-ups like Shield, Invisibility, Speed Boost, and Silencer.

Phantom Strike offers multiple game modes — Classic Mode, Time Attack, Zen Mode, Challenge Mode, and rotating Daily Missions — plus a Lucky Spin wheel, Daily login rewards, star-based level progression, Achievements, and global Ranks. It's engaging, fun, and completely FREE.

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the Phantom Strike Android application. It describes what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. 📋 Information We Collect

a) Device Information

  • Device Advertising ID (used for ad targeting)
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data)

b) Gameplay Data (Anonymous)

  • Level reached, moves used, and star ratings earned
  • Game mode performance (Classic, Time Attack, Zen, Challenge, Daily Mission)
  • Coins and diamonds collected and spent
  • Power-ups purchased and used (Shield, Invisibility, Speed Boost, Silencer)
  • Lucky Spin results and Daily reward claims
  • Achievements unlocked and rank progress
  • Game crashes or errors (for app improvements)
  • Basic app performance metrics

c) Advertising ID

  • Google Advertising ID — used by ad networks to serve relevant ads
  • This is anonymous and resettable by you anytime
✅ What we DON'T collect: your name, email, or phone number · your location or GPS data · credit card or payment information · any personally identifiable information · your browsing history or contacts · any data about your device usage outside this game.

3. 🚫 What We DON'T Collect

To be crystal clear, Phantom Strike does NOT collect:

  • Personal Information — We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any personal details
  • Financial Data — No credit card information is ever requested or stored by us (optional purchases, if any, are handled securely by Google Play)
  • Location Data — We don't access your GPS, location services, or whereabouts
  • Contact Information — We never access your contacts, SMS messages, or call logs
  • Photos or Media — We don't access your photos, videos, or media library
  • First-Party Analytics — We don't use internal tracking to monitor your individual behavior
  • Calendar or Reminders — We don't access your calendar, reminders, or personal schedules
  • Account Credentials — You don't need to login with any account to play
✅ Result: Phantom Strike respects your privacy. Just install it, play it, strike from the shadows. No strings attached.

4. 📢 Advertising Networks

Phantom Strike is a completely free game. To support development, we display advertisements within the app — including rewarded ads that grant bonus coins, extra Lucky Spins, or hints — using two advertising networks:

📌 Important: We use exactly 2 advertising networks in Phantom Strike: ✅ Google AdMob   ✅ Unity Ads

📊 Google AdMob

Google's mobile advertising platform. AdMob serves banner and interstitial ads within Phantom Strike.

Data AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Google Ad ID)
  • IP address (country/city level geotargeting)
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • Ad interaction signals (views, clicks)
  • Network type (Wi-Fi / Mobile)
📖 Privacy Policy ⚙️ Opt Out

🎮 Unity Ads

Unity's in-game advertising platform. Unity Ads serves rewarded video ads (for earning bonus coins, spins, or hints) and interstitials to support the game's free distribution.

Data Unity Ads may collect:

  • Unity Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS)
  • Ad interaction signals
  • General location (country/city)
📖 Privacy Policy
✅ How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:
Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on your Android device. You can also reset your Advertising ID, which will show you less targeted (more generic) ads.
⚠️ Important: The data collected by Google AdMob and Unity Ads is governed entirely by their own privacy policies (linked above). Lihka does not have access to, control over, or receive any personally identifiable information from these networks. We only receive basic aggregate revenue data.

6. 🧒 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Phantom Strike is appropriate for all ages, including children.

✅ Child Safety Measures:

  • The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13
  • Google AdMob is configured with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) compliance
  • No account registration is required to play
  • In-game coins and diamonds are earned through gameplay, Lucky Spin, and rewarded ads
  • No external links to potentially unsafe content
  • Age-appropriate ads and content only
  • Rank leaderboards show only player usernames (no personal data)
If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately at support@lihka.in and we will delete it promptly.
