1. 🎓 About CAD Quiz

CAD Quiz is a comprehensive quiz and learning application for practicing CAD (Computer-Aided Design) software commands and skills. The app is developed and operated by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

CAD Quiz provides an interactive platform to:

  • 📝 Take practice tests on AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Siemens NX, G-Code, and M-Code
  • 📚 Learn and master CAD commands with detailed explanations
  • 📊 Track your progress and test scores
  • 🎯 Test your knowledge in multiple languages (English, Hindi, Marathi)
  • 🏆 Improve your skills with difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard)
This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the CAD Quiz Android application. By using the app, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. 🛡️ Our Key Privacy Principles

Everything we build in CAD Quiz is guided by these core privacy principles:

  • ✅ Local-First Data: All quiz questions, your test answers, progress, and test history are stored entirely on your device.
  • ✅ Minimal Data Collection: We collect only the minimum anonymous device data required for the app to function and display advertisements. We do not collect your name, email, or personal account information.
  • ✅ No Cloud Upload: Your quiz data, test results, and progress are never uploaded to any server — ours or any third party's.
  • ✅ No Personal Profiling: We do not build personal profiles of users. We do not track what you practice or how you answer questions.
  • ✅ Transparency: Every permission we request is explained clearly in this policy.

3. 📋 Information We Collect

a) Information We Do NOT Collect

✅ We do NOT collect:

• Your name, email address, or phone number
• Your test answers or quiz scores
• Your learning progress or history
• Your preferred difficulty level or language
• Which CAD software you practice
• How long you spend on quizzes
• Any personally identifiable information
None of this data is transmitted from your device.

b) Anonymous Device Information (Collected Automatically)

The following minimal, anonymous technical data may be collected automatically for app stability monitoring and displaying advertisements:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Android OS version
  • App version number
  • Anonymous Advertising ID (resettable by you)
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data) — for ad loading only
  • General geographic region (country/city level — for ads only)
  • App crash logs (anonymous — no personal data included)

c) No Account Required

CAD Quiz requires no registration, no login, no email address, and no personal account of any kind. The app functions fully based on your device's local storage.

📌 Important: Your quiz data, test results, and progress are stored entirely on your device's local storage. Lihka does not have access to and does not transmit any of your quiz data.

4. 📱 Permissions We Request & Why

We request only permissions strictly necessary for the app to function. Here is a complete breakdown of every permission used in CAD Quiz:

🌐 INTERNET
Required for: Loading banner advertisements from Google AdMob to support the free app. All quiz features, questions, and progress tracking work fully offline without an internet connection.
📶 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Required for: Checking your internet connection status before attempting to load advertisements.
📢 AD_ID Advertising Only
Required for: Google AdMob to serve banner advertisements. This is an anonymous, resettable Advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out of personalized ads anytime via: Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.
⚠️ Note: CAD Quiz does not request or use Location (GPS), Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or any other sensitive permissions.

5. 📂 How We Use Your Information

The minimal anonymous data we collect is used only for the following purposes:

  • ✅ Displaying banner advertisements via Google AdMob to support the free app
  • ✅ Anonymous crash reporting to identify and fix app bugs
  • ✅ Improving overall app performance and stability
🚫 We NEVER:

• Access, read, or transmit your quiz answers or test results
• Upload your test data or progress to any server
• Build a personal profile of you
• Sell your data to any third party
• Share personal information with advertisers
• Track you in the background when the app is closed

6. 📢 Advertising — Google AdMob

CAD Quiz is a free app. To support development and maintenance, we display banner advertisements within the app using Google AdMob only.

📌 Important: We use exactly 1 advertising network: Google AdMob. No other ad networks are used.

What data does Google AdMob collect?

📊 Google AdMob
Google's mobile advertising platform. AdMob may collect anonymous data including your Advertising ID, IP address (country/city level), device model, OS version, and app interaction signals to serve relevant banner ads within CAD Quiz.

AdMob does NOT have access to: Your quiz answers, test results, progress history, or any quiz content.
✅ How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset Advertising ID   or   Opt out of Ads Personalization

7. 🔒 Data Security

We take data security seriously. The following measures are in place:

  • 🔐 All quiz data is stored locally on your device — no transmission to servers
  • 🔐 No user accounts or user databases maintained on our servers
  • 🔐 Only minimal anonymous diagnostic data may be transmitted securely via HTTPS
  • 🔐 No third-party tracking SDKs beyond disclosed Google AdMob
  • 🔐 All ad communication uses secure HTTPS connections
⚠️ Device Security Reminder: Since all your quiz data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device security settings. We recommend setting a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or biometric) on your device.

8. 💾 Data Storage & Retention

Your Quiz Data:

  • Stored entirely on your device's local storage
  • Retained until you delete it manually within the app or via device settings
  • Deleted automatically when you uninstall the app
  • Lihka has no access to or copy of any of your quiz data

Anonymous Diagnostic Data:

  • Crash logs retained for a short period to diagnose bugs, then deleted
  • No personally identifiable information is included
✅ Complete Data Deletion: To remove all quiz data from your device:
  • Delete your quiz history within the app
  • Clear app data: Settings → Apps → CAD Quiz → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app from your device
