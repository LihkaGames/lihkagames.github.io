🎮 About Glass Velocity

Glass Velocity is a fast-paced, action-packed game where you break glass obstacles, dodge challenges, and race against time. With progressive difficulty, dynamic levels, and smooth physics-based gameplay, it delivers non-stop entertainment for casual and competitive gamers alike.

Created by Lihka (based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India), Glass Velocity is built with a privacy-first philosophy. We believe great gaming doesn't require compromising your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Glass Velocity collects, how we use it, and what rights you have. Our commitment: minimal collection, maximum protection, complete transparency.

ℹ️
Your consent matters. By using Glass Velocity, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you have concerns, you can uninstall anytime. We'd love feedback to improve!

🚫 Data We Absolutely Don't Collect

Your privacy is sacred. These things are completely off-limits:

❌ Personal Information

We never ask for or store:

  • Names, usernames, or display names
  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Home address or physical location
  • Birth date or age (except COPPA checks)
  • Social security numbers or financial data

❌ Sensitive Data

We never access or collect:

  • Location tracking or GPS coordinates
  • Contacts, phone book, or messaging
  • Photos, videos, or files from your device
  • Calendar events or reminders
  • Social media accounts or credentials

❌ Online Activity

We never track:

  • Browsing history or web activity
  • App usage outside Glass Velocity
  • Search queries or search history
  • Downloaded files or documents

❌ Biometric & Advanced Data

We never collect:

  • Fingerprints or facial recognition
  • Iris scans or biometric identifiers
  • Health or fitness data
  • Behavioral psychology or personality profiles
✅
Your privacy is guaranteed. Glass Velocity doesn't need personal information to work great. Play with complete confidence!

Opt Out of Personalized Ads

Don't want personalized ads? You have full control:

✅
How to disable personalized ads:
1. Go to your phone Settings → Google → Manage your Google account
2. Select "Data & Privacy" tab
3. Find "Ad personalization" and toggle it OFF

You can also reset your Advertising ID anytime in Settings → Privacy → Ads

✏️ Your Privacy Rights

You have legal rights regarding your personal data under various privacy laws including GDPR, CCPA, and others. Here's what you can do:

📋 Right to Access

Request to see what personal data we've collected about you and how it's used.

🗑️ Right to Delete

Request permanent deletion of your personal data from our systems.

✏️ Right to Correct

Request correction or update of inaccurate or incomplete data we hold.

🚫 Right to Opt-Out

Opt out of personalized ads anytime through your device settings.

📤 Right to Portability

Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.

⚖️ Right to Object

Object to certain types of data processing or profiling.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@lihka.in with:

  • 📌 Your request clearly stating which right you're exercising
  • 🪪 Proof of identity (we handle sensitive requests carefully)
  • 📝 Any details that help us locate your specific data

Response Time: We'll respond to verified requests within 7 business days. Most requests are handled much faster. If we can't fulfill a request, we'll explain why.

ℹ️
We'll never charge for exercising your rights. Data access, deletion, and correction requests are always free.
