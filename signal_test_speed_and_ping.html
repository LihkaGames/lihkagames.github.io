About Signal Test: Speed & Ping

Signal Test: Speed & Ping is a network diagnostics utility developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. It helps you measure download/upload speed, ping & jitter, scan nearby WiFi networks, monitor live bandwidth usage, and review your test history with analytics charts.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, why it's needed, and how you stay in control. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

ℹ️ This policy covers the Signal Test: Speed & Ping Android app only. It does not apply to other Lihka apps.
2

Data We Collect

📱 Device & Network Information

  • Device model, Android version
  • Active network type (WiFi / Mobile Data) & carrier name
  • Advertising ID (anonymous, resettable)
  • Basic crash & performance logs

⚡ Test Results (Stored On Device)

  • Download / Upload speed results (Mbps)
  • Ping, jitter & packet loss values
  • Server/host pinged (e.g. google.com, 8.8.8.8)
  • Timestamped test history & performance grade
  • WiFi scan results — SSID, signal strength (dBm), channel, frequency band
✅ All test results and history shown in the History and Analytics screens are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal test history to our servers.
3

Location & WiFi Scanning — Explained

To show you nearby WiFi networks in the WiFi Analyzer feature (signal strength, channels, network names), Android requires apps to hold a Location permission. This is an Android OS-level security requirement — not something we choose for tracking purposes.

📍 What this means for you:
• Location permission is used solely to retrieve WiFi scan results.
• We do not access GPS coordinates.
• We do not store, log, or transmit your location anywhere.
• You can deny this permission — the WiFi Analyzer simply won't be able to list nearby networks, but speed & ping tests still work fully.

Local Storage & History Export

Your Test History and Analytics charts (speed-over-time, ping history, average grade) are generated entirely from data stored locally in the app's database on your device.

  • You can delete individual tests via the Delete button in History
  • You can wipe everything instantly via Clear All
  • You can Export your history as a file for your own records
  • Uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally stored data

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: in-app advertising
