1. 🎮 About ToonCrosser

ToonCrosser is an addictive, action-packed mobile game developed by Lihka, based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

The game challenges you to guide adorable cartoon characters safely across busy roads and railways while avoiding traffic. Featuring multiple game modes including Classic Mode, Time Attack, Zen Mode, Survival Mode, and Challenge Mode. Unlock unique character skins by earning coins through gameplay and lucky spins. It's engaging, fun, and completely FREE.

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the ToonCrosser Android application. It describes what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. 📋 Information We Collect

a) Device Information

  • Device ID (used for ad targeting)
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data)

b) Gameplay Data (Anonymous)

  • High scores and game statistics
  • Game mode performance (Classic, Time Attack, Zen, Survival, Challenge)
  • Coins collected and earned
  • Character skins unlocked and selected
  • Spin wheel rewards claimed
  • Daily gift status
  • Game crashes or errors (for app improvements)
  • Basic app performance metrics

c) Advertising ID

  • Google Advertising ID — used by ad networks to serve relevant ads
  • This is anonymous and resettable by you anytime
✅ What we DON'T collect:

❌ Your name, email, or phone number
❌ Your location or GPS data
❌ Credit card or payment information
❌ Any personally identifiable information
❌ Your browsing history or contacts
❌ Any data about your device usage outside this game

3. 🚫 What We DON'T Collect

To be crystal clear, ToonCrosser does NOT collect:

  • 🚫 Personal Information — We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any personal details
  • 🚫 Financial Data — There are zero in-app purchases. No credit card information is ever requested or stored
  • 🚫 Location Data — We don't access your GPS, location services, or whereabouts
  • 🚫 Contact Information — We never access your contacts, SMS messages, or call logs
  • 🚫 Photos or Media — We don't access your photos, videos, or media library
  • 🚫 First-Party Analytics — We don't use internal tracking to monitor your individual behavior
  • 🚫 Calendar or Reminders — We don't access your calendar, reminders, or personal schedules
  • 🚫 Account Credentials — You don't need to login with any account
✅ Result: ToonCrosser respects your privacy. Just install it, play it, enjoy it. No strings attached.

4. 📢 Advertising Networks

ToonCrosser is a completely free game. To support development, we display advertisements within the app using two advertising networks:

📌 Important: We use exactly 2 advertising networks in ToonCrosser:
✅ Google AdMob
✅ Unity Ads
📊

Google AdMob

Google's mobile advertising platform. AdMob serves banner and interstitial ads within ToonCrosser.

Data AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Google Ad ID)
  • IP address (country/city level geotargeting)
  • Device model and Android OS version
  • Ad interaction signals (views, clicks)
  • Network type (Wi-Fi / Mobile)
📖 View Google's Privacy Policy →    ⚙️ Opt Out of Personalized Ads →
🎮

Unity Ads

Unity's in-game advertising platform. Unity Ads serves rewarded video ads (for earning coins and unlocking characters) and interstitials to support the game's free distribution.

Data Unity Ads may collect:

  • Unity Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS)
  • Ad interaction signals
  • General location (country/city)
📖 View Unity's Privacy Policy →
✅ How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:

You can opt out of personalized ads on your Android device at any time through:

Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

You can also reset your Advertising ID, which will show you less targeted (more generic) ads.
⚠️ Important: The data collected by Google AdMob and Unity Ads is governed entirely by their own privacy policies (linked above). Lihka does not have access to, control over, or receive any personally identifiable information from these networks. We only receive basic aggregate revenue data.

6. 🧒 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ToonCrosser is appropriate for all ages, including children.

✅ Child Safety Measures:

• The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13
• Google AdMob is configured with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) compliance
• No account registration is required
• No in-app purchases means no financial transactions
• No external links to potentially unsafe content
• Age-appropriate ads and content only
• Leaderboards show only player usernames (no personal data)

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately at support@lihka.in and we will delete it promptly.
